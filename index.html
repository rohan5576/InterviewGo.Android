🔒 Privacy Policy

InterviewGo ("we", "us", or "our") is an Android interview preparation app that helps you discover interview questions, read community experiences, browse job listings, and prepare for technical interviews. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Content notice: InterviewGo displays interview questions, company information, job listings, and salary ranges that may be collected from public websites, community sources, and user submissions. We do not independently verify all of this information. See the Disclaimer tab for more details.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address, display name, username, and password (stored in encrypted/hashed form)
  • Profile information: Company or college name, job designation, and optional avatar
  • User content: Interview experiences, round details, questions, answers, notes, votes, bookmarks, and feed posts
  • Support messages: Information you send when contacting us

Information Collected Automatically

  • Authentication data: Sign-in timestamps and session tokens managed by our backend provider
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens used to deliver notifications you opt into (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Crash diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports to help fix app stability issues (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Subscription status: Premium plan status and purchase references processed through Google Play

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Important: Experiences, questions, comments, and other content you post may be visible to other users. Do not share confidential employer information, proprietary code, trade secrets, or NDA-protected details.

InterviewGo also displays information that does not come directly from you, including:

We do not guarantee that this information is accurate, complete, current, or officially endorsed by any employer or third party. External job links may redirect you to other websites governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share information only with service providers needed to operate the app and when required by law.

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and backend infrastructure
  • Google Firebase: Push notifications and crash reporting
  • Google Play: In-app subscription billing and payment processing

6. Data Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods
  • Access to production systems is limited on a need-to-know basis

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion

  • Opt-out: Disable notifications or delete your account at any time

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA (California), or similar laws. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data: Until you delete your account

  • Push tokens and notification preferences: Until you disable notifications or delete your account

9. Children's Privacy

InterviewGo is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
